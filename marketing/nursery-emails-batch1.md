# Nursery Outreach - Initial Three

Send individually from `Sanju | Bloom Juniors <sanju@bloomjuniors.com>`. Do not send
the remaining nursery list until this test produces response data.

## Shared pilot details

- Focus: children aged 3-5, with progression available through age 9
- Nursery receives: parent pack, setup support, aggregate 30-day summary and review
- Privacy: no child email accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases or data sales
- Accurate reporting term: "anonymised, aggregate engagement summary"

---

## 1. Yellow Kite Nursery

**To:** operations@yellowkitenursery.com  
**Subject:** A 30-day home-learning pilot for Yellow Kite families

Dear Yellow Kite team,

I am reaching out because Yellow Kite has a strong family-centred presence in Safa 2.

I'm Sanju, a Dubai parent. I built Bloom Juniors (bloomjuniors.com) for my own
daughter: a free, ad-free home-learning app with systematic synthetic phonics,
early maths and stories. It is designed for ages 3-9, with this nursery pilot
focused on children aged 3-5.

Children do not need email accounts. There are no advertisements, in-app purchases
or data sales. Learning progress is stored securely so families can continue where
they stopped and see what their child has practised.

I'm inviting three Dubai nurseries to test a supported 30-day pilot. We provide the
parent message, setup support and an anonymised, aggregate engagement summary, then
review the results with you. Families keep free access after the pilot.

Would you be open to a 15-minute demonstration next week? I can visit on Tuesday or
Thursday morning.

Warm regards,

Sanju  
Founder, Bloom Juniors  
sanju@bloomjuniors.com | bloomjuniors.com
