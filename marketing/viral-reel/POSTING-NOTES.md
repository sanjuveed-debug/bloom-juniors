# Founder Question Reel

## Caption

I carried this question from childhood: why does the Sun look red at sunset?

Nobody explained it in a way I could see or test. So when I built Bloom Juniors
for my daughter, I wanted children to predict first, watch what happens, and
discover the reason for themselves.

What question did you keep asking as a child? Tell me below. I may turn it into
the next Wonder.

Free for ages 3-9 at bloomjuniors.com
