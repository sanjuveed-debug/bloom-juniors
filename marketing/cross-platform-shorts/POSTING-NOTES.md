# Parent Short Series

These are clean 9:16 masters for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
Upload the MP4 directly to each platform; do not download and repost a version
with another platform's watermark.

The `*-windows-preview.wmv` files are local viewing copies for Windows Media
Player. Do not upload the WMV copies; publish the corresponding MP4 masters.

## Publishing Rules

1. Address parents in every title, caption, and opening frame.
2. Upload the clean master independently to each platform.
3. Use only audio owned by Bloom or licensed inside the destination platform.
4. Publish three strong vertical videos per week, not multiple low-quality
   posts every day.
5. Record views, engaged views, average watch time, comments, shares, saves, and
   profile visits after 24 and 72 hours where each platform exposes them.
