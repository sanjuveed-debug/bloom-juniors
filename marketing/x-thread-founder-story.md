**1/**
I counted once: 14 ads in 10 minutes inside a "free educational" app my daughter was using.

One of them was for a diet product. She was four.

That was the night I opened my laptop and started building.

**2/**
Why is the sun red at sunset? Why are leaves green? Why do things fall?

I had questions like these as a kid and nobody had time to answer them properly.

I didn't want that for my daughter either.

**3/**
So I built Bloom Juniors: British curriculum phonics and maths for ages 3-9.

Then I added "Wonder Why" — one real question a day, a prediction first, a tiny experiment, a Wonder Book that remembers every discovery.

Predict first. Then discover. Not "memorize the answer."

**4/**
No ads. No premium wall. No coins to buy.

Free means something different when a dad builds it for his own kid instead of a growth team building it for a board. It means free, forever.

**5/**
It's live at bloomjuniors.com — no download, works in any browser.

What question did YOU keep asking as a kid that nobody properly explained? Tell me below — I might turn it into the next Wonder.
