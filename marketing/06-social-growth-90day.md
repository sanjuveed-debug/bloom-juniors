# Bloom Juniors — 90-Day Social Growth Strategy

Calibrated to reality, not the generic version: solo founder, zero ad budget, PWA (no app
store — the conversion target is bloomjuniors.com, not an install), and hard evidence from
the last 30 days that aggressive/automated posting backfires (X flagged the account for
"inauthentic behavior" after rapid automated posting; 33 Instagram posts produced 2,250
views and 0 followers). This is a secondary channel behind the nursery B2B pipeline, run at
a pace one person can sustain by hand — not a daily content factory.

---

## 1. Audience Persona

**Primary: Dubai/UAE parents of kids aged 3-9, British-curriculum households**
- Dual-income or single-income expat families, value structured early-years education, likely already paying nursery/school fees in the AED 10k-40k/year range
- Psychological triggers:
  - **Screen time guilt** — the highest-performing content so far (the "kid grabbed my phone" trend post) hit this directly
  - **Falling-behind anxiety** — comparing their child's readiness against peers before school entry
  - **Ad/tracking fatigue** — burned before by apps that are secretly monetizing their kid's attention or data
  - **Time scarcity** — want something that works in 10 minutes without them managing it
- What converts them: proof it's actually ad-free/tracking-free (not just a claim), a specific relatable moment (not generic "learning app" messaging), and permission to feel like a good parent for choosing 10 structured minutes over open-ended screen time

**Secondary: nursery principals** — already covered by the B2B outreach kit; not this document's focus, but content pillar 5 below exists specifically to feed that pipeline with social proof.

---

## 2. Content Pillars (5)

1. **"Guilty Screen Time → Guilt-Free"** — the pain point that already worked (screen-time-guilt trend post). Parent-relatable moments about screen time, reframed around Bloom as the guilt-free alternative.
2. **"Yaagvi Moments"** — mascot-led short clips showing the app in action, warm/playful tone, low production cost since it reuses the existing character asset.
3. **"Founder, Building in Public"** — Sanju's story as a Dubai dad who built this for his own daughter. Already started on X; extend lightly to Instagram. This is the one pillar that doesn't need new production, just repurposing.
4. **"Real Progress, Real Parents"** *(gated — don't start until nursery pilots produce real families)* — actual parent testimonials/UGC once there's a pilot nursery with real usage. Don't fabricate this with actors or stock content; wait for it to be real.
5. **"Nursery Partner Spotlight"** *(gated — same condition)* — once a pilot closes, content co-branded with that nursery. This is also the asset that makes the next round of nursery outreach easier (social proof), so it's worth prioritizing production time on this the moment a pilot exists.

Pillars 4 and 5 are deliberately gated behind actual pilot outcomes — posting fake-feeling testimonials before they're real would cost more trust than it builds.

---

## 3. Platform Plan (scoped to solo bandwidth)

**Instagram — primary, Reels only, 2-3x/week (not daily).** This is where the account and Meta Verified status already live. Cadence is capped deliberately: the automation-driven daily posting attempted in this project produced zero follower conversion, so more volume alone isn't the fix — spacing out and improving hook quality is worth testing before increasing frequency again.

**X — secondary, manual/human-paced only, 2-3x/week max, no batch/automated posting.** The account is still under a reach-limiting flag from automated back-to-back posting. Do not resume any scripted rapid-fire posting pattern here; post individually, spaced out, like a person would.

**YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn — not started.** Cross-posting the same Reels to YouTube Shorts is low-cost once Instagram content exists (same asset, different upload) and can be added later without new production. LinkedIn is skipped entirely for now — it's a B2B/investor-facing platform and the founder-story content is better spent where actual parents and nurseries are, not where GTM advice is (that audience is what this conversation itself is already serving).

## 5. Micro-Influencer Outreach (Dubai parenting creators, 5k-50k followers)

Target creators, not "study-gram" accounts (wrong audience) — Dubai/UAE mum/parenting lifestyle accounts with genuinely engaged, local followings. Offer: free full access (already free, so frame as early/founder access + being featured), no payment expected at this stage — this is a relationship, not a paid placement.

**DM/email template:**

> Hi {Name}, I'm Sanju — a Dubai dad who built Bloom Juniors, a free, ad-free British EYFS learning app for my own daughter (phonics, early maths, stories, ages 3-9, no ads or tracking ever). I love how you talk about {something specific to their content — a real detail, not generic}, and thought your audience might genuinely find this useful, not just as a sponsored post but as something worth trying with their own kids. No ask attached — just wanted to share it in case it's useful, and if you ever try it and have thoughts, I'd love to hear them.

Keep this list small (5-10 creators) and personal — this only works if it reads as genuine outreach, not a mail-merge.

---

## 6. Conversion Funnel (PWA, not app store)

Since Bloom Juniors is a web app, not a native app store listing, the funnel target is different from a typical ASO playbook:

- **Bio link**: bloomjuniors.com directly, not a linktree — one destination, no friction
- **Pinned comment / first comment**: repeat the link on every post (IG doesn't allow clickable links in captions)
- **CTA in-video**: verbal/text callout to "try it free at bloomjuniors.com" in the last 2 seconds of every video, not just the caption
- **On-site**: make sure the landing page has an "Add to Home Screen" prompt for PWA installs, since that's the actual equivalent of an app store install for this product — worth confirming this exists and is prominent (can check the codebase if useful)
- **Incentive**: no artificial incentive (discount codes etc.) needed since the product itself is already free — the CTA is trust-building ("no ads, no tracking, no cost"), not urgency-building

## What NOT to do (calibration notes, not part of the original template)

- Don't return to daily/batch automated posting on X — that's what triggered the current reach restriction
- Don't fabricate Pillar 4/5 content before real pilot data exists
- Don't chase this harder than the nursery pipeline — this stays a 2-3x/week background motion, not the main GTM bet
