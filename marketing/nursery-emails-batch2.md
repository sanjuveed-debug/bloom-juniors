Dear Ms. Turner,

I am reaching out because Willow is exactly the kind of independent, parent-focused nursery I'm hoping to partner with in Umm Suqeim.

I'm Sanju, a Dubai parent. I built Bloom Juniors (bloomjuniors.com) for my own
daughter: a free, ad-free home-learning app with systematic synthetic phonics,
early maths and stories. It is designed for ages 3-9, with this nursery pilot
focused on children aged 3-5.

Children do not need email accounts. There are no advertisements, in-app purchases
or data sales. Learning progress is stored securely so families can continue where
they stopped and see what their child has practised.

I'm inviting a small number of Dubai nurseries to test a supported 30-day pilot. We provide the
parent message, setup support and an anonymised, aggregate engagement summary, then
review the results with you. Families keep free access after the pilot.

Would you be open to a 15-minute demonstration next week? I can visit on Tuesday or
Thursday morning.
