# Nursery Pitch Video - Script for Google Flow/Veo

**Length:** 35-40 seconds  
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 landscape  
**Audience:** Nursery principals and owners  
**Tone:** Credible, warm and founder-to-professional

## Shot 1 (0-5s) - The need

**Visual:** Yaagvi waves beside a simple classroom board on a clean background.  
**On-screen text:** "A supported home-learning pilot for your families"  
**Voiceover:** "Every nursery wants a strong partnership with families. Bloom
Juniors gives parents one short, guided learning journey at home."

## Shot 2 (5-15s) - What families receive

**Visual:** Yaagvi taps softly glowing letter and number tiles; a book opens beside
her. Keep the scene uncluttered and use slow, deliberate movement.  
**On-screen text:** "Phonics | Early maths | Stories | Ages 3-9"  
**Voiceover:** "Built by a Dubai parent, Bloom Juniors combines systematic synthetic
phonics, early maths and stories in an ad-free app designed for ages three to nine."

## Shot 3 (15-24s) - Child-first design

**Visual:** A checklist appears one line at a time.  
**On-screen text:** "No child email accounts" / "No ads or in-app purchases" /
"No data sales"  
**Voiceover:** "Children do not need email accounts. There are no advertisements,
in-app purchases or data sales."

## Shot 4 (24-32s) - The nursery pilot

**Visual:** Parent-message, setup and aggregate-report icons connect in one simple
three-step line.  
**On-screen text:** "Parent pack | Setup support | Aggregate 30-day summary"  
**Voiceover:** "For the 30-day pilot, we prepare the parent message, support setup
and share an anonymised engagement summary before reviewing the results with you."

## Shot 5 (32-40s) - The ask

**Visual:** Yaagvi waves; fade to the Bloom Juniors logo and website.  
**On-screen text:** "Book a 15-minute demonstration | bloomjuniors.com"  
**Voiceover:** "Families keep free access after the pilot. Book a fifteen-minute
demonstration and help us make home learning work better for your community."

## Production notes

- Use static shots, slow pushes and clean cross-fades.
- Do not show generated text inside device screens; add exact text in post-production.
- Do not claim KHDA approval, inspection evidence or that no child data is stored.
- No real children or real nursery classrooms without explicit releases.
- Use calm instrumental music without lyrics, kept below the voiceover.
